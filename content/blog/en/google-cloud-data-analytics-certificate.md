I just earned the **Google Cloud Data Analytics Certificate**, issued by Google Cloud. The verified badge is up on [Credly](https://www.credly.com/badges/4d168010-1e64-4d0b-adfd-27b4b0a2baa5/public_url).

## What it covers

The certification focuses on the full data workflow on Google Cloud: ingesting and transforming data, running efficient queries with **BigQuery**, and visualizing results to support decision-making. It's a natural complement to the serverless architecture microcredential I earned on AWS earlier this year — same idea, different cloud.

## Why I went for it

Between university and my internship at Indra ATM Sistemas, most of my hands-on work has been at the application and infrastructure layer. I wanted to round that out by actually understanding the data layer: how data gets modeled, queried and put to use at scale in a managed cloud environment, instead of treating it as a black box behind a dashboard.

## What's next

It's already reflected in the [Education & Certifications](/en/cv) section of the portfolio. I'll keep pairing this with hands-on data projects as time allows — for now it's one more step logged along the way.
